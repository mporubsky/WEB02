What

Forms of care

All-day care

Monday to Friday, 7:30 – 17:30.

We are preparing, among other things: storytelling afternoons, exercise sessions for children and their mothers, callanetics for mothers (with the children looked after), craft workshops, trips, Sunday get-togethers, birthday parties, talks and discussions, a weekend English school for pre-schoolers and barbecues in the summer…

Who

Qualified teaching staff

How we choose them:

  • Education — a university degree in education
  • Character — a real way with children, empathy, creativity
  • Languages — a long stay abroad or years of language study
  • Experience — in international kindergartens, or other specialised schools, for instance for gifted children

We will be glad to answer any further questions or comments. We will gladly tell you more on request.
