Our philosophy

A defining feature of EU education policy is raising the quality of education — which is our aim too.

Whatever the state of schooling in Slovakia may be, the need parents have to find a place with a pleasant atmosphere and the best possible conditions for their little one is very much here. It is an important and demanding step. That is probably why we have met on these pages. We are glad you approach bringing your child up responsibly and with love.

“A state school, or something alternative and private? If only it were in English as well… and in very small groups, where they really can give my little one their time… and…”

By opening our kindergarten we are trying to help fill in the blank spots. We want to offer what we know how to do well, in the conditions our children deserve. We offer an alternative — if you are choosing a kindergarten for your little one but would rather have a family-run private one, with language teaching, very small groups, an individual approach, a pleasant atmosphere, colourful rooms, the option of further courses for children (tennis or swimming, for instance) and extra services for you, the parents…

What we intend: to offer a complete service of real quality. To offer you, the parents, the best for your children — for their bright eyes today and a happy future tomorrow.

Part of our philosophy is to find the potential in every child and to develop it as far as it will go, through play. We put the emphasis on the child's whole personality and creativity, and we guarantee an individual approach to each one — in a pleasant atmosphere the children build together with professional, experienced teachers. Language teaching by a natural method, and being at ease with a computer, we see as necessary for a successful future, so both are a matter of course in our programme.

We follow the approved Slovak Kindergarten Programme of 1999 as an open document setting out the basic direction, and add to it the newest thinking from research and practice at home and abroad.

Quality

High quality in teaching and care is secured by

  • choosing teachers carefully — for what they know, but for who they are as well
  • guaranteeing low numbers of children per group
  • using alternative teaching methods
  • good teaching materials and equipment
  • working internationally — building contacts with partner schools abroad
  • an individual approach and support for gifted children
  • developing IQ and EQ alike, the whole personality, and creativity and social skills in particular
  • supporting each child's particular abilities as far as they will go
  • environmental, moral and multicultural education, and education towards a healthy way of life

The aim of the language teaching is a conversational command of the language.

Modern trends

Of the modern trends in education, we follow

  • an individual approach
  • working with small groups of children
  • a family atmosphere
  • developing personality, emotional intelligence and creativity
  • a social curriculum — practice in handling everyday situations, conflicts above all, and learning responsibility and how people behave together
  • an emphasis on character — encouraging the child towards critical thinking, values and morals
  • education towards a healthy way of life, and environmental education
  • learning first of all through play, experiment and hands-on activity
  • co-operative teaching
  • learning foreign languages
  • being at ease with a computer
  • multicultural education
Play

Play has an important place here

We see it as the means by which the separate parts of a child's personality are deliberately developed.

Creative play

Gives the child a chance to express itself and to feel the joy of doing so, which builds independence, motivation and imagination, teaches it to solve problems and introduces it to the feeling of responsibility.

Didactic play

Brings knowledge above all.

Educational play

Puts the emphasis on living something through, and on experience.

We assume a good education for your child matters to you. If we think alike about bringing children up and the conditions we offer suit you — or if you would like to know more about us — please call or e-mail us.

We will be glad to build, together with you, a world in which your child is happy and you are content.
